Platform Capabilities

What every LeadOne agent ships with

Regardless of which agent you deploy, it comes with the same platform underneath — these are the pieces that make it production-ready.

Real-time transcripts & recordings

Every conversation is transcribed and recorded as it happens, searchable right after the call ends.

Calendar-verified booking

Appointments are checked against real-time availability, and always saved — with or without a connected calendar.

Multi-tenant workspaces

Run separate workspaces for teams, brands, or clients, each with its own agents and data.

Live analytics dashboard

Call outcomes, volume, and usage, tracked in real time — not a weekly export.

Configurable voice & script

Tune voice, tone, and talking points per agent — no two deployments have to sound the same.

Do-not-call & compliance safeguards

Built-in guardrails honor do-not-call requests and keep every call within policy.

Natural barge-in

Callers can interrupt and be heard instantly — no waiting for the agent to finish talking.

Works with or without a calendar

No Google Calendar connected yet? Appointments still save straight to your workspace.
